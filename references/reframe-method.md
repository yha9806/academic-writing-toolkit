# Reframe Method: Engineering Report → Paper Form

Salvaged from the retired `/manuscript-reframe` skill, stripped of its
original project's domain vocabulary. Use when a draft reads like a module
inventory or validation packet rather than a scientific argument.

1. **Find the spine.** Write the one sentence the paper exists to defend.
   If sections cannot be ordered as steps in defending it, the structure is
   an inventory, not an argument.
2. **Name the gap in the field's words**, not the system's ("X cannot yet
   be done reliably", never "we built module Y").
3. **Recast contributions as answers to the gap** — each one falsifiable,
   none a component name.
4. **Convert the results narrative from "what we ran" to "what it shows":**
   every table/figure gets one job stated in its caption's first sentence;
   a figure whose job cannot be stated is cut or moved to an appendix.
5. **Move engineering detail** that supports no claim into appendices or a
   repository pointer.
6. **Draw the AI-assistance boundary explicitly** if agents drafted or
   revised text or analysis: what was machine-produced, what the author
   verified (see `references/evidence-vocabulary.md`).
7. **Write limitations from the pre-mortem**, not from convention
   (see `references/argument-checklist.md`).
8. **Readiness check:** spine sentence present in abstract, intro, and
   conclusion without broadening; every claim type-matched to evidence; no
   section that only describes machinery.
