# A Small Demonstration Paper

## Abstract

This paper proves that short reflective prompts universally improve academic
writing. We asked twelve volunteer students in one seminar to use a prompt
before revising a paragraph. Eleven students said that the prompt felt useful.
The study therefore establishes that the method works for all disciplines and
should replace conventional writing support.

## Method

Twelve volunteers from one postgraduate seminar completed the activity. We did
not use a comparison group, blind assessment, or an independent writing-quality
measure. Participants answered one question about perceived usefulness.

## Discussion

The positive responses show that reflective prompting causes better writing.
Future work may test the method in other disciplines and with direct measures of
revision quality.
