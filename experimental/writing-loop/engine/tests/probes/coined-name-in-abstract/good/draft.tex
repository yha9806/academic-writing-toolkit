\title{When the gauge drifts before the flood: Sensor drift as an early warning}
\begin{abstract}
We built GaugeBench, a record of 14 river gauges, and read sensor drift against chance. Drift rose before 12 of 14 floods, an early warning at each gauge.
\end{abstract}
